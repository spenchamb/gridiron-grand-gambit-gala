Keepers

Every team's kept player, season by season.

 

Each team protects one keeper per season. A player can be kept at most twice — three seasons on a roster — before he must be released back to the draft. The tag under each name shows his roster tenure (year 1–3) and how many times he's been kept; a player in his 3rd season (kept twice) is in his final keeper year and must be released next season.
