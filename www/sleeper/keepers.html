Keepers

Every team's kept player, season by season.

 

Each team keeps one player per season — the player entered as their Round 1 keeper on draft day. A player can be kept at most twice (three seasons on a roster) before he must be released back to the draft. The tag under each name shows how many consecutive years that team has kept him; a 2nd-year keeper is in his final keeper season.
