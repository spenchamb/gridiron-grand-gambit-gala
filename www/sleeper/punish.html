Punish Watch

Who's on track to finish with the fewest wins?

 

Punish Watch tracks the race nobody wants to win: fewest regular-season wins. Each team's projected final wins starts from their current win total and adds a win probability for every remaining game, based on their All-Play win% against each upcoming opponent's All-Play win%. It's a projection, not a lock — treat it as a guide to who's most at risk, not a guarantee. If two teams finish tied in wins, the league breaks the tie by total points scored during the regular season (fewer points loses the tiebreak).
