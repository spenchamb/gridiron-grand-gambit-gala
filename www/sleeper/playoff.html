Playoff Watch

Who's on track to reach the playoffs?

 

Playoff Watch tracks the race to the top 6. We simulate the rest of the season — each remaining game decided by resampling both teams' real weekly scores this year — and rank the final standings by wins, breaking ties on total points scored. Each team's Playoff % is how often that simulation lands them in the field. It captures schedule strength and scoring variance, not just current record — so a team a game back can still be the favorite.
