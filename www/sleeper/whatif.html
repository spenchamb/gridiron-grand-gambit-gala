Alternate Realities

What-If

Re-run each season under different rules.

 

Scoring Systems — same lineups, different scoring

Full PPR reproduces the league's real recorded scoring exactly (to the point); half PPR and standard then remove 0.5 and 1.0 points per reception, using the lineups each manager actually started. Regular season only. PF = points for.

If No Trades Happened

Median Format — beat the weekly median for a bonus win

Each week you play your scheduled opponent and the league median score. Win both for 2-0, split for 1-1. Rewards consistent scoring and removes schedule luck. Records are out of double the games.

All-Play Record — each week vs the entire league

Every week you play all other teams at once — a win for each team you outscore. This strips out schedule luck entirely, so it's the fairest read on week-to-week scoring. Records are out of many games (weeks × opponents), regular season only.

Best Ball — optimal lineup every week

Standings if every manager auto-started their optimal lineup each week (no start/sit decisions), using real per-player league scoring, replayed against the actual schedule. Pure roster strength — the gap vs actual is start/sit skill (or luck).
